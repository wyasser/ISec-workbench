Control library · app version

Controls follow the five functions and 23 domains of the CBE Egypt Financial Cybersecurity Framework, assessed across people, process and technology. Requirement texts are paraphrased assessment statements, not official CBE wording, and are mapped to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0 and CIS Controls v8 for reference. Validate them against the current official framework; the CBE is preparing a second edition. AI suggestions are assistive only; the assessor decides every status.
